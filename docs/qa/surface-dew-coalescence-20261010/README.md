## Rules and fixed work

Two fixed bead slots share one route generated by the original
`SurfaceTriangleWalker`. Neighboring beads approach along connected triangle
barycentric coordinates. A distance check gates cubic-volume transfer:

```
contactDistance <= 1.08 * life * (rA + rB)
VA = rA^3 + merge * rB^3
VB = (1 - merge) * rB^3
rMerged = cbrt(rA^3 + rB^3)
```

Surface ellipsoid axis product is `.58*r^3`; actual geometric volume differs
only by the shared `4π/3` factor. The elongating attached shapes and the rounded
flight shape keep the same volume before their explicit fade. The larger bead
absorbs its neighbor; the second slot becomes zero scale.

Each seeded period is 9–15 seconds. Normalized cycle ages are condensation
`.06–.28`, approach `.30–.70`, contact transfer `.42–.50`, and release from `.66`.
A merged radius at least `.020 * unit` can detach from a side/downward face with
world normal y <= `.3`. Upward-facing faces retain and evaporate the large bead
instead of releasing a drop that gravity would return into its source plane.
Small beads evaporate at `.77–.93`. Empty slots wait until new condensation in
the next cycle.

At first release, the current actual posed triangle supplies the contact and
world normal. The new rounded ellipsoid's exact plane support places it outside
that triangle. Launch origin, velocity and volume are frozen in world space;
later body movement, rotation and nonuniform scale do not move the falling drop.
Velocity includes a small outward component and a negative world-y component;
gravity is `.7 * unit` downward. Flight lasts at most 1.15 seconds, fading at
`.9–1.15`, and never casts an opaque particle shadow.

There is one instanced mesh, one owned sphere geometry and one owned physical
transmission material. Actual render pass count includes Three's transmission
and scene passes. Construction retains the old maximum 1,152/1,920 seed attempts
and prioritizes routes touching the preserved arm morph before halving the old
route pool into pairs. Sparse surfaces retain fewer distinct routes. There is no
all-pairs neighbor search or added physics system. At most four world launches
per route are cached for pause/reverse seek. Reset/reattachment clears the cache;
a cold seek uses the supplied current pose because past body poses are unavailable.

Attached beads resolve current source vertices and geometric normals on every
update. Local accessory masks test the full bead envelope, including affine
transform bounds; release-source and moving-flight regions are also checked.
Equipping/unequipping changes neither seeds nor launch history. Owned instance
buffer, geometry and material release once; source resources remain caller-owned.

## Limits and publication boundary

This is bounded pair coalescence and detached gravity flight, rather than a fluid
simulation. Source-face clearance and accessory envelopes are verified; continuous
collisions with every nearby body part or ground, topology/skinning changes,
extreme poses and scale changes are not implemented. Existing rest-frame routes
do not recalculate world-downhill surface flow after display rotation. A cold seek
cannot reconstruct an unavailable earlier body pose.

Native Chromium SwiftShader views were inspected, including close-up separation,
growth and falling, and the posed fitted-mask outfit. Physical desktop/mobile
GPU measurements and a hosted logged-in run were not performed. No additional
Site deployment, main merge, PR, authentication/permission change, external asset
retrieval, or animal fabrication is authorized or performed in this task. Existing
Site v31 and the fashion/core branches remain unchanged. The final candidate is
normal-pushed to its independent private GitHub branch and checked back separately.
